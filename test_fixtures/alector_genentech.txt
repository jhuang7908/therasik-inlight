Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On September 30, 2026, Alector, Inc. (the "Company") entered into a Collaboration 
Agreement (the "Agreement") with Genentech, Inc. ("Genentech"), a member of the 
Roche Group. Pursuant to the Agreement, the Company granted Genentech an 
exclusive, worldwide license to develop, manufacture and commercialize antibody 
products targeting progranulin ("PGRN") and one additional target.

Under the terms of the Agreement, the Company will receive an upfront payment 
of $100 million in cash upon the execution of the Agreement. The Company is 
eligible to receive up to an aggregate of $1.17 billion in potential development, 
regulatory and commercial milestone payments. Additionally, Genentech will pay 
tiered royalties on worldwide net sales ranging from mid-single digits to low 
double digits.

The Company expects that the upfront payment and potential milestone payments 
will extend its cash runway into 2029.
