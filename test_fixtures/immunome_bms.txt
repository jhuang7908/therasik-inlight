Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On October 2, 2026, Immunome, Inc. (the "Company") entered into Amendment No. 4 
(the "Amendment") to the License and Collaboration Agreement, dated as of October 2018, 
as amended (the "Original Agreement"), with Bristol-Myers Squibb Company ("BMS").

Pursuant to the Amendment, the Company paid BMS $20.0 million in cash and issued 
4,425,487 shares of the Company's common stock to BMS. In consideration for such 
payments, the Amendment removes the Company's obligation to make any milestone 
and royalty payments to BMS under the Original Agreement. Following execution of 
the Amendment, the Company will retain full rights to develop and commercialize 
products under the Original Agreement without any further payments to BMS.

The Amendment simplifies the Company's capital structure and eliminates future 
contingent payment obligations that would have been payable to BMS upon the 
achievement of certain development and commercial milestones.

Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities

The Company issued 4,425,487 shares of common stock to BMS as described above.
