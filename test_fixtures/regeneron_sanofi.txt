Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On October 1, 2026, Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("Regeneron" or the "Company") 
entered into a Sixth Amendment (the "Amendment") to the Antibody License and Collaboration 
Agreement, dated as of January 2007, as amended (the "Collaboration Agreement"), with 
Sanofi S.A. ("Sanofi").

Pursuant to the Amendment, Sanofi will pay Regeneron an upfront payment of $1.0 billion 
in cash. Additionally, the Company is eligible to receive up to an aggregate of $7.0 billion 
in development, regulatory and commercial milestone payments contingent upon the achievement 
of certain specified events. The Amendment also provides for the transfer of exclusive 
rights for four antibodies currently in clinical development.

The parties agreed to modify the profit sharing arrangement, with profits to be shared 
equally between Regeneron and Sanofi for products developed under the collaboration. 
The Amendment includes a budget cap for certain development activities and an option 
for lunsekimig, subject to further negotiations. As part of the Amendment, the parties 
agreed to dismiss certain pending litigation matters.
