Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On September 30, 2026, Rocket Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the "Company") entered into 
a Loan and Security Agreement (the "Credit Agreement") with Hercules Capital, Inc. 
("Hercules"), as administrative agent and collateral agent, and certain lenders 
party thereto.

The Credit Agreement provides for term loan commitments in an aggregate principal 
amount of up to $150.0 million, consisting of five tranches. The first tranche of 
$35.0 million (Tranche 1-A) was funded on September 30, 2026. The second tranche 
of $20.0 million (Tranche 1-B) is available for draw within 180 days following 
the closing date. The remaining tranches are subject to achievement of certain 
milestones.

In connection with the Credit Agreement, the Company issued to Hercules warrants 
to purchase 1,755,853 shares of the Company's common stock at an exercise price 
of $2.99 per share. The warrants are exercisable for a period of ten years.

The term loans bear interest at a variable rate equal to the greater of (i) Prime 
Rate plus 3.0% and (ii) 11.0% per annum.

Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities

The Company issued warrants to purchase 1,755,853 shares of common stock to 
Hercules as described above.
